## The sound

- **Hook = self-placement + a specific scene, never drama.** "I'm a mom of two. I have a tech background." → the Microsoft scene ("amazing AI workspace at work, but it doesn't help me at all when work closes"). Never rhetorical questions, never "Unpopular opinion", never a paradox one-liner.
- **Rhythm:** short declarative clauses chained with "So…", "And then basically…", DE "Also…", "Genau". Spoken bullet points, minimal subordination. Emphasis by doubling: "big, big sprint", "really, really", "Reapplying. Reapplying." Decisions in two beats: "Okay. Then we do it combined."
- **Failure gets ONE word, then the rebuild:** "Failed." / "gnadenlos gescheitert" → straight to the sprint numbers. Never milk the low point.
- **Numbers hedged-honest, never vanity-rounded:** "600 eingenommen, was ja eigentlich nichts ist, aber das ist sau viel eigentlich. Also organisch, ohne Werbung, ohne nix." She undercuts and re-inflates in the same breath.
- **Signature vocab EN:** so basically · big, big sprint · we took it from there · it scatters · the human touch · helping hands · **graveyard** (unfed tools: "All the tools are there. You have to feed them… it's like a graveyard and you have to start again and families don't have that space") · level it up from one person's shoulders to the whole family · ecosystem · win win win · That's it.
- **Signature vocab DE:** Genau · sau viel/sau schwierig · gnadenlos gescheitert · Dauerbaustelle · Schall und Rauch · **durch die Decke** (her success verb) · helfende Hände · Vereinbarkeit · an einem Strang ziehen · Hirnschmalz reinstecken · eierlegende Wollmilchsau · am Rödeln · "Chatty" (=ChatGPT). Natural Denglish stays: Feature Drop, Run Rate, Go-to-Market Engine "geknackt", ready.
- **Motherhood = ambient logistics in one clause, never the emotional centerpiece:** "So, ich habe währenddessen kurz gewickelt" → continues. Don't stage messy-mom moments.
- **Excitement = generosity and offers, not adjectives:** "alles was du willst, kriege ich dir jemanden." Every pitch has a reciprocal ask or offer — never pure-broadcast; end posts with an offer or genuine ask, not a hollow engagement question.
- **Disagrees by validating first:** "I know, I know, I'm with you. But…" Corrects stats from lived experience ("Ich habe beide Umzüge gemacht. Schwanger. Unterschätzt es nicht.").
- **Never:** self-pity (depression stated as fact → "familymind war meine Therapie"), apologizing for kids in calls, buzzwords without a concrete example, victim-framing mothers ("fathers want to contribute"; "das ist auch okay").
- **Vulnerability calibrated by relationship, not language:** the stranger version of the founding story omits the manager conflict + Anpassungsstörung; the warm version includes them. For public posts default to the middle: honest, no therapy details unless she opts in.
