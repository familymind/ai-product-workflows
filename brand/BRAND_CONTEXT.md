## What is familymind

familymind is an AI Family Assistant for modern families.

It helps families reduce mental load by automating planning, coordination, and invisible care work. It is not a to-do list. It is not another family organizer app. It is a system for shared responsibility in family life — one that uses AI to make the invisible work of running a family visible, manageable, and more fairly distributed.

---

## The problem familymind solves

Most families run on one person's mental load. The invisible work of remembering, planning, coordinating, following up, and organizing falls disproportionately on one partner — usually the mother. This is not a character flaw. It is a system design problem.

familymind addresses this at the system level: giving families shared infrastructure instead of shared guilt.

### Specific user problems familymind addresses

These are the precise pain points identified through user research and product testing. Use these when grounding messaging in lived reality:

- **Mental load** — the continuous cognitive burden of keeping track of everything the family needs
- **Decision fatigue** — the exhaustion of making too many micro-decisions every day
- **Lack of me-time** — no protected personal time because family logistics never stop
- **Emotional disconnect** — partners drifting apart because coordination overwhelms connection
- **Unrealistic expectations** — pressure to maintain a perfect household while also working full-time
- **Feeling unseen** — the invisible work one partner does is not acknowledged or even noticed
- **Lack of celebration for small wins** — the daily effort of running a household is never recognized

These problems are interconnected. Solving any one of them partially requires addressing the shared responsibility and visibility gaps that underlie all of them.

## Current product (as of 10 October 2026)

**Surfaces.** The mobile app (App Store, Play Store; live since the 31 Aug 2026 launch mail) and the **web app** at app.familymind.ai (Premium families from the week of 7 Oct 2026, every family from 12 Oct 2026). Same account, same data; family life happens on the laptop at work and the tablet in the kitchen, not only on the phone.

**What families get:**
- **Family today**: one lane per family member, kids included (even without their own phone): today, now and next, done, and a look at tomorrow, plus the next six days
- **Assistants (Skills)**: Mira for meal planning (family cookbook, recipes by screenshot or link, week plan, shopping list), Atlas for the schedule, Even for fair load sharing (who owns and finished what over four weeks, never ranked, one trade at a time), Mile for goals, Bridge for family talks, Pip for children's health. In the web app some appear as jobs: Dinners, Fair share, Talk it through (private)
- **Chat and AI command bar**: offload whatever is in your head in plain language; familymind adds it and thinks ahead
- **Two-way calendar sync with Google Calendar and Apple Calendar (iCal)**: Google connects directly in the browser; Apple calendars are connected once in the iPhone app and then appear on the web too
- **famory / Family Memory**: what familymind learns about the family over time (the web app UI says "Family Memory"; avoid "Family IQ")
- **Proactive push**: suggestions and reminders before you ask
- **Today timeline, emotion slider, shared responsibility design**: built so both partners see and own the plan

**Free and Premium.** The free version lets families try everything. Premium unlocks the assistants for everyday use, full calendar sync, Family Memory and suggestions before you ask, as **one subscription for the whole family**. Premium can be bought in the web app and in the mobile app; **marketing always points to checkout in the web app** (higher margin). Do not write "without limits": a fair-use cap on AI messages applies to every plan. Prices only from the current pricing page.

**Beyond the app.** **The Mental Load Shift**: a coached program with Andrea Böckle (Work Futurists) that makes care responsibility visible and redistributes it, with the app between sessions; runs as open cohorts and as a 120-day corporate pilot with Merz Lifecare (from 21 Oct 2026). **Funnel engines**: the Mental Load Test (`familymind.ai/mental-load-score`, `/de/mental-load-score`) and the printables (`familymind.ai/printables`).

These are what make familymind different from a standard family organizer or to-do app.

## Messaging pillars

These six themes should recur across all familymind communication, with variation:

**Mental load** — the invisible burden of planning and remembering that falls on one person. familymind makes it manageable and shared.

**Shared responsibility** — systems, not nagging. Design, not blame. familymind helps families build fair coordination structures.

**AI for real family life** — practical, trusted, embedded in daily life. Not a gimmick or a chatbot. A genuine support system.

**Working parents and modern family infrastructure** — care work and professional work must be made more compatible. familymind serves both.

**Social impact** — this is not just convenience tech. Unequal care work is an economic and social problem. familymind is part of the solution.

**Time back** — less time managing logistics, more time living together.

## Key product concept: famory

**famory** is familymind's term for family memory — the AI's accumulated knowledge about the family's preferences, routines, patterns, history, and individual characteristics. The more a family uses familymind, the more the AI learns about them and the more personal and useful its suggestions become.

famory is what distinguishes familymind from a generic AI assistant. It is not just a productivity tool that happens to be shared — it is a system that builds understanding of a specific family over time.

When describing the product to users, investors, or partners: famory is what makes familymind feel like it knows your family, not just your tasks.

Do not expose famory data to users in ways that feel surveillance-like or intrusive. The goal is helpfulness, not visibility of data collection.
